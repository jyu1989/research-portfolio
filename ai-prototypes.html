AI PRODUCT BUILDING · RAPID PROTOTYPING

Exploring AI Interactions Through Practical Prototypes

Two hands-on applications built with AI-assisted development tools to explore how useful AI features can adapt to everyday contexts.

← Back to selected work

Prototype 1 — On-the-go workout coach

User need: help someone decide what exercises to do spontaneously, even without a gym. I used Replit to build a workout coach that takes context such as the room or outdoor setting and available equipment, including dumbbells, a mat, or a chair, into account when making suggestions.

Design consideration: the recommendation needs to respond to real constraints rather than assume a fixed workout plan or full gym setup.

Prototype 2 — Baby-cry analysis

User need: support parents trying to interpret infant crying. I used Lovable to prototype an app for recording and analyzing crying and providing recommendations.

Design consideration: AI-generated interpretations of crying are uncertain and should not be represented as diagnoses or definitive assessments of an infant's needs.

What I learned

AI-assisted development lowered the barrier to turning a research or everyday-life idea into a usable prototype. It also raised product questions about how to communicate uncertainty, contextualize recommendations, and test whether users find the system helpful and trustworthy.

These are exploratory prototypes. No formal usability study, validation metrics, or deployment scale is claimed. Screenshots and live links can be added once reviewed.
