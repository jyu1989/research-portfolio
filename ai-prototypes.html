SIDE PROJECTS · AI-ASSISTED DEVELOPMENT

Building tools for everyday problems

I've been learning to build apps with Replit and Lovable. These two prototypes began with problems I wanted to solve for myself, from finding time to exercise to understanding an upset baby.

01 · REPLIT

On the Spot

A workout coach for the time and space you actually have.

Some days I have ten minutes, a bedroom, and a pair of dumbbells. I wanted an easier way to start moving without searching for a workout or following a rigid plan.

Choose your contextWhere you are, how you feel, what equipment is nearby, and how long you have.
Get movingRequest a workout suited to those inputs.
Keep a recordThe interface includes strength logs and daily movement goals.

What I explored: designing around people's immediate constraints instead of asking them to adapt to a fixed routine.

Actual interface, built in Lovable

02 · LOVABLE

Hush

A concept for helping parents respond to a crying baby.

When a baby is upset, it's not always obvious what to try next. I prototyped an interface where parents can share a photo or short video, receive possible explanations and age-appropriate soothing suggestions, and report what worked.

CaptureTake a photo or a short video.
Consider possibilitiesExplore potential causes and soothing steps, presented as suggestions rather than diagnoses.
Give feedbackRecord what helped, with personalization as a product goal.

What I explored: communicating uncertainty and designing a feedback loop for personalized guidance.

WHAT I'M LEARNING

From an idea to something I can test

Building these apps has helped me understand AI-assisted development from the product side: defining a user problem, making interface decisions, and identifying what would need to be tested before people could rely on the output.

These are exploratory prototypes. I have not conducted formal usability studies or validated the accuracy of Hush's suggested interpretations or the effectiveness of either app.
