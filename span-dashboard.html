SPAN: Building a Survey Platform for Organizational Research

I co-founded SPAN to turn the specialized needs of organizational researchers into a configurable survey platform. Researchers have used it to conduct relational, organizational network, and employee studies. The platform also includes feedback workflows that translate survey responses into interpretable results.

The problem: relational research needs specialized survey workflows

Conventional questionnaires do not always support the organizational hierarchies, respondent relationships, peer nominations, and network questions needed for organizational research. We developed SPAN to make these studies easier to configure and administer.

My contribution as a co-founder and builder

I helped define what the platform needed to do, translated research requirements into product features, designed survey and feedback workflows, and worked with software engineers to bring those features into a functioning platform. This was collaborative product development, not a solo software build.

Impact: used by researchers

SPAN has been used as a survey platform by researchers conducting organizational network and employee research. Its contribution is practical research infrastructure that supports specialized data collection and feedback workflows, beyond a demonstration prototype.

Product experience: Peer and self-feedback

In addition to research survey administration, SPAN includes employee-facing feedback screens combining multidimensional scores, organizational benchmarks, construct descriptions, and tailored suggestions.

Researcher-facing configuration

  • Build organizational hierarchy
  • Create Likert, network, and filter scales
  • Build questionnaires and research projects
  • Assign respondents, raters, and ratees

Platform screens

Reusable research workflow

Feedback, not just data export
