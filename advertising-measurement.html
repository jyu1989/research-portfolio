01 / THE RESEARCH QUESTION

Which advertising attributes provide meaningful information?

Across different advertising creatives, the team examined how attributes related to ad liking, purchase intent, and brand outcomes. The challenge was translating survey relationships into evidence that could support interpretation and future measurement decisions.

Consulting outcome

Delivered analysis, visualizations, and guidance on interpreting effect sizes, overlapping predictors, heterogeneity, and measurement quality. The engagement led to follow-up consultation on measurement and scale development.

Technical methods and research limitations Expand details

Analyses: Creative-level regression, random-effects meta-analysis, and multilevel models for repeated or clustered ratings; measurement and factor-structure checks.

Methodological judgment: Considered multicollinearity, response quality, higher-level sample size, scale development, effect-size interpretation, and the information loss caused by dichotomizing continuous responses.

Interpretation: Survey associations are observational and should not be interpreted as causal advertising effects. The outcome described here is consulting analysis and methodological guidance, not a measured campaign lift.
